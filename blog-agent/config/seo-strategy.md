## On-page rules (apply to EVERY post)

1. Target keyword in the H1 (title) and naturally in the first 100 words.
2. Use a secondary keyword in at least one H2 if it fits naturally — never stuff.
3. Location/market context present only where it is real and relevant to the topic — not
   forced onto every post regardless of subject.
4. Specific metrics, not vague claims (99.2%, 120/min, 70%, sub-second — not "high accuracy").
5. Meta description under 160 chars, includes the target keyword.
6. End with a direct CTA relevant to the post's actual vertical — Free AI Audit, or a direct
   ask tied to the subject. Do not default to an inspection-line CTA on a non-CV post.
7. Minimum 1,400 words, ideally 2,000+. Real data/numbers always beat generic claims.

## Technical SEO (handled by the schema step + site)

- Article (BlogPosting) JSON-LD on every post — generated by the swarm's schema step.
- FAQPage JSON-LD when the post answers discrete buyer questions — generated by the schema
  step.
- Internal links: every post links to a relevant service page and, where it overlaps, a
  published post.
