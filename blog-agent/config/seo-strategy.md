## THE MANDATE (2026-09-03): one cluster until 25 posts

The blog writes **manufacturing computer vision / quality control**. Nothing else.

Measured over 38 posts and 112 days: 1,899 impressions, 17 clicks — and two posts held
92.7% of that. The 29 matured posts were spread across 8 clusters, none near the ~25-post
threshold where one interlinked cluster starts to rank. Manufacturing-CV has 14 posts and
the only page-2 page. It is the one with a chance.

Out of scope for NEW posts until this cluster reaches 25: document AI, AI agents, workflow
automation, AI web apps, ecosystem/corridor pieces. **The company still sells all five.
The blog does not chase all five at once.**

## Target ONE keyword per post, from this cluster

- computer vision quality control
- automated visual inspection manufacturing
- manufacturing defect detection AI
- machine vision companies india
- computer vision QC system cost
- vision inspection vs manual QC
- build vs buy computer vision system

## Every keyword must clear the demand gate

`swarm/demand` measures the target keyword BEFORE research. A phrase with no measurable
demand is rejected, not written. "AI precision agriculture India" ran to 2,517 words,
cleared every other gate, and earned zero impressions. Do not invent keyword-shaped
phrases — check that people type them.

## Intent mix: 60-80% bottom-of-funnel

Informational queries trigger an AI Overview 36% of the time; commercial 8%;
transactional 5%. When one shows, organic CTR is ~0.6%. So write what buyers search:

- comparisons — "X vs Y", vendor shortlists, "top N companies for <use case>"
- cost — real figures and ranges, never "it depends"
- build vs buy, how to evaluate, what to ask a vendor before signing
- case studies carrying the actual numbers (99.2%, 94%, 120 items/min, sub-second)

Authority and explainer pieces: at most 1 post in 5.

## On-page rules

1. Target keyword in the H1 and inside the first 100 words.
2. Specific metrics, never vague claims.
3. Meta description under 160 chars, containing the keyword.
4. Link to the cluster pillar and at least one sibling post in this cluster.
5. 1,400-2,000 words. Depth beats length.
