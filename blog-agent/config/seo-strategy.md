## On-page rules (apply to EVERY post)

1. Target keyword in the H1 (title) and naturally in the first 100 words.
2. Use a secondary keyword in at least one H2 if it fits naturally — never stuff.
3. India / Chennai / Tamil Nadu context present where relevant (location SEO is a gap to own).
4. Specific metrics, not vague claims (99.2%, 120/min — not "high accuracy").
5. Meta description under 160 chars, includes the target keyword.
6. End with a direct CTA: "Talk to us about your inspection line" or Free AI Audit → buteforce.com/lp/ai-audit.
7. Minimum 1,400 words, ideally 2,000+. Real data/numbers always beat generic claims.
