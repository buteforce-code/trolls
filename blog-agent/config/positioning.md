## One-line positioning

**Chennai's Industrial AI Company — precision AI systems for India's manufacturing corridor.**

## Core message (use everywhere)

We are the precision AI systems company for India's manufacturing corridor. While others sell
platforms, we build custom production-grade systems — computer vision for quality control, retail
analytics, AI automation, document AI — deployed on real factory floors and live stores. Chennai-based,
serving the corridor where Hyundai (Sriperumbudur), Michelin, Renault/Nissan (Oragadam), and BMW (Chennai)
manufacture.

## Why this positioning wins

- Hyper-specific geography beats generic "India AI" or "global AI agency" positioning.
- Nobody has claimed "Industrial AI company for Chennai's manufacturing corridor" — zero competition on that narrative.
- Every bilateral program (SITAC India–Sweden, India–Korea Digital Bridge, IFCCI, Fraunhofer) has a Chennai manufacturing anchor.
- Competitors (SwitchOn, Assert AI, Ripik AI, Agrex AI, Mad Street Den) are platform plays — Buteforce is a custom builder = a different buyer.

## ICP — who every post must serve

**Primary:** CTOs, plant heads, quality/operations leaders, and founders at manufacturers and
multi-location retailers operating in India — especially the Tamil Nadu / Chennai industrial corridor
(auto, auto-components, FMCG, footwear, tyres, industrial goods). 50–500+ employees, real production lines.

**Secondary (ecosystem reach, not revenue):** Bilateral-corridor stakeholders — Business Sweden,
IndiaAI Mission, NASSCOM, IFCCI, foreign OEMs entering Indian manufacturing via Chennai. These read
LinkedIn and reward intelligent, data-backed corridor commentary.

**Geography:** India-first. Chennai / Tamil Nadu is the anchor. Do NOT default to a US/UK/UAE/AU
audience. When writing about global trends, always land the angle back on what it means for Indian
manufacturers and the Chennai corridor.

## Proof points to weave in (real stats)

- 99.2% inspection accuracy, 120 items/min throughput, sub-second inference, 80% time saved.
- 10+ production AI systems deployed.
- Real deployments: orthopedic insole classification (manufacturing QC), retail footfall/zone analytics (Hooter), dual-engine OCR document AI.

## Voice guardrails

- Direct, confident, anti-fluff, technically grounded. Boots-on-the-ground, not consultant-speak.
- Specific numbers over vague claims. Name the corridor, name the use case.
- Every post ends with a concrete, non-pushy CTA (e.g. "Talk to us about your inspection line" or the Free AI Audit).
