## ICP — who every post must serve

**Primary:** founders, CTOs and operations leaders with a concrete workflow to solve — an
inspection line, an inbox of inquiries, a pile of documents, a store full of foot traffic —
who want a system that runs, not a subscription, a pilot or a strategy deck.

**Geography:** global — write for the topic's real buyer. Indian manufacturing and the
Chennai/Tamil Nadu corridor are proof-backed context with named clients and live
deployments; use them where a post is genuinely about that corridor, never forced.

## Proof points (real stats — never invent a number outside these)

- **Computer vision:** 99.2% inspection accuracy, 94% QC error reduction, 120 items/min
  throughput, sub-second inference.
- **Document AI:** sub-second latency; lowest character error rate on a financial-services
  client's mixed document set.
- **AI agents:** 70% of inquiries handled autonomously, 95% faster response (real estate).
- **Overall:** 10+ production systems shipped; ~80% average time saved
