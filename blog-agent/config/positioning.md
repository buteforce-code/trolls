## One-line positioning

**Precision AI Systems — custom AI that works from day one. No consultants, no pilot
projects, no fluff.**

## Do not name

Do not name or reference **EasyBali** in any blog content — no exceptions currently in
force. The underlying capability (full-stack AI web applications, booking/concierge flows)
can still be described generically; the product name specifically must not appear.

## Core message (use everywhere)

Five capability areas, each with real shipped proof — no vertical is the flagship, none is
the default:

- **Computer vision** — object detection, classification, tracking (YOLOv8 + DeepSORT). QC
  and defect detection on production lines, footfall/zone analytics for retail and
  construction, people tracking. Productised as **Hooter**.
- **Document AI & OCR** — dual-engine (Mistral 7B + Google Cloud Vision) processing of
  printed, handwritten, and complex-table documents at sub-second latency.
- **AI agents** — autonomous agents for lead qualification, inquiry handling, and 24/7
  customer support across WhatsApp, web, and email.
- **Workflow automation** — end-to-end process automation: listing automation, content
  pipelines, document-to-database, outreach sequences, multi-system integration.
- **AI-powered web applications** — full-stack builds with AI at the core: conversational
  interfaces, booking systems, recommendation engines.

Manufacturing computer vision is where the first hard numbers came from — well-proven, not
exclusive. Treat it as one strong vertical among several, not the company's identity.

## ICP — who every post must serve

**Primary:** Founders, CTOs, and operations leaders with a concrete, describable workflow to
solve — an inspection line, an inbox full of inquiries, a pile of documents, a store full of
foot traffic — who want a system that runs, not a platform subscription, a pilot, or a
strategy deck. Spans manufacturing, retail, real estate, financial services, e-commerce, and
travel/hospitality.

**Geography:** Global. No default audience and no excluded one — write for whoever the
topic's actual buyer is. Indian manufacturing and the Chennai/Tamil Nadu corridor remain
real, proof-backed context (named clients, real deployments) — use it where a post is
genuinely about that corridor, never forced onto other verticals or markets.
