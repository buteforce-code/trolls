# SITAC — India-Sweden AI Corridor Opportunity

**Status:** ACTIVE — First-mover window open (corridor is only 4 months old)  
**Priority:** HIGH

## What Is It
The Sweden–India Technology and Artificial Intelligence Corridor (SITAC) was signed February 25, 2026 between the IndiaAI Mission and Business Sweden. It is a government-backed bilateral AI corridor connecting Indian and Swedish companies, startups, and institutions.

## Why Buteforce Fits
- Manufacturing QC (YOLOv8, 99.2% accuracy) = SITAC priority: Industrial AI
- Hooter (retail CV analytics) = Swedish retailers IKEA India, H&M India
- Document AI = supply chain + FinTech applications
- Indian company = directly aligned with IndiaAI Mission goals
