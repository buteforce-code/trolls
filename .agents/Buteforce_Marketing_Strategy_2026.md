## 5. LINKEDIN STRATEGY

### The fundamental insight (2026 algorithm)
- **Personal profile (Dhyan's) gets 8x more engagement than ButeForce company page**
- **Carousels: 45.85% engagement rate — top format**
- **Comments worth 15x more than reactions**
- **First 60 minutes of a post are critical — engage with every comment immediately**
- Founder-led content beats corporate content every single time

### ButeForce Company Page — Role: Credibility Signal
Post 3x per week. Content:
- Blog post reposts (every blog post becomes a company page post)
- Case study stats (one number, one outcome, one CTA)
- Bilateral program updates (SITAC, IndiaAI news) with Buteforce angle added
- Job/project wins (without client name if needed)
- Product feature updates (Hooter, new capabilities)

### Dhyan's Personal Profile — Role: Revenue Driver
Post 4-5x per week. Content types:

**Type 1: Founder Story (highest engagement)**
Format: "I [did X]. Here's what I learned:"
Example: "I deployed our first computer vision system on a live manufacturing line with 0 ground truth data. Here's what broke first:"
→ Real technical + emotional story. Comments flood in.

**Type 2: Contrarian Take (algorithm boost from debate)**
Example: "Hot take: 99% of 'AI for manufacturing' companies are selling dashboards, not automation. There is a difference. Thread:"
→ Gets challenged. Comments = algorithmic reach.

**Type 3: Data Post (high save rate)**
Format: One counterintuitive number + explanation + takeaway
Example: "We analyzed 120 items per minute across 10,000 product images. The YOLOv8 model accuracy wasn't our hardest problem. The lighting was. Here's how we solved it:"

**Type 4: Bilateral Corridor Post (visibility with officials)**
Example: "SITAC was signed 3 months ago. Here's what an Indian AI startup actually needs to participate in it: [numbered list]"
→ Gets seen by Sofia Högman, Kavitha Bhatia, NASSCOM officials. Inbound > outbound.

**Type 5: Carousel (highest format engagement)**
Make one carousel per week. Topics:
- "5 AI use cases in manufacturing that actually have ROI in India"
- "How to spec an AI quality control system (step by step)"
- "The Chennai manufacturing corridor: 6 companies, 6 AI opportunities"
- "SITAC explained for Indian AI startups in 6 slides"

### Hashtag strategy (LinkedIn)
Primary (use every post): #IndustrialAI #ComputerVision #ManufacturingAI
Secondary (rotate): #Chennai #IndiaAI #AIautomation #MachineVision #RetailAnalytics
Bilateral: #SITAC #IndiaSweden #IndiaKorea #IndiaFrance #MakeInIndia

- Twitter: 8am–12pm IST (peak global window) — schedule posts at 8:30am and 11am

## 11. THE BILATERAL CORRIDOR CONTENT PLAY (Special)

This deserves its own section because it's the highest-leverage channel for getting into rooms like SITAC and IndiaAI without cold emails.

**The mechanism:** Government officials, Business Sweden staff, IndiaAI Mission team, NASSCOM program managers — they all monitor LinkedIn for Indian startups talking intelligently about their programs. When a company post gets shared by P. Thiaga Rajan (Tamil Nadu Finance Minister, 41K followers, already posted about SITAC) or tagged by Sofia Högman — it's worth 100 cold emails.

**How to trigger this:**
1. Write the SITAC blog post (Post 6 in plan above)
2. Write the SITAC carousel (6 slides)
3. Post both and tag: @Business Sweden India page, @IndiaAI Mission page, mention Sofia Högman (@ her post about SITAC as a comment, not a tag — less spammy)
4. Post the "Chennai Manufacturing Corridor" piece and tag: relevant Tamil Nadu government pages, @NASSCOM, @YourStory (media)
5. When officials share/comment: respond immediately and professionally

**What to write that gets attention from these people:**
- Specific, data-backed posts (not generic "AI is changing manufacturing")
- Posts that acknowledge the program + add new information or perspective
- Posts that identify a specific problem the program should solve (shows expertise)
- Never: promotional content, "please select us", overly salesy framing
