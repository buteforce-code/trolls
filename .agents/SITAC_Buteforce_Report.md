## Section 1: What Is SITAC?

**Full Name:** Sweden–India Technology and Artificial Intelligence Corridor  
**Launched:** February 25, 2026 — India AI Impact Summit, New Delhi  
**Signed between:** IndiaAI Mission (Government of India, under MeitY) ↔ Business Sweden (Sweden's trade and investment promotion authority)  
**Nature:** Statement of Intent — a formal framework for structured bilateral AI cooperation. Not a treaty, but carries government mandate.

### What It Does
SITAC is the flagship bilateral platform that connects government agencies, industry stakeholders, startups, and academic institutions from both India and Sweden to co-develop, deploy, and scale AI solutions. It is organized around:
- Conferences and thematic workshops
- Ecosystem exchanges between Indian and Swedish AI companies
- Field visits to innovation hubs in both countries
- Engagement among companies, investors, researchers, and policymakers
- Identification of joint innovation platforms and investment corridors

### Why It Matters Right Now
PM Modi visited Sweden in 2026, upgrading India-Sweden ties to a **Strategic Partnership** with a Joint Action Plan 2026–2030 targeting to double bilateral trade from $7.75B to ~$15.5B. SITAC is the tech/AI arm of this. Money, attention, and political will are actively being deployed.
