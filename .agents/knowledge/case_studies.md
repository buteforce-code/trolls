## Case Study 1: Computer Vision — Manufacturing QC

**Client:** Manufacturing company (orthopaedic insole production)
**Problem:** Manual quality inspection — slow, inconsistent, error-prone
**Solution:** Custom YOLOv8 model trained on 10,000+ product images, deployed on live production line

### Results
- ✅ 99.2% classification accuracy
- ✅ 120 items/minute processed
- ✅ 94% reduction in QC errors (first month)

### Tech Stack
YOLOv8 | PyTorch | OpenCV | Industrial cameras
*(Full stack context → [[tech_stack]])*

### Quote
> "They shipped a working computer vision system in three weeks. Our quality control errors dropped by 94% in the first month. I didn't think it would be this fast."
> — Operations Director, Manufacturing Client

## Case Study 2: Document AI — OCR Pipeline

**Client:** Financial services company
**Problem:** Manual data extraction from diverse document types (printed, handwritten, complex tables)
**Solution:** Dual-engine OCR system routing between Mistral 7B and Google Cloud Vision

### Results
- ✅ Sub-second processing latency (<1s)
- ✅ Lowest character error rate achieved
- ✅ Handles any document format — printed, handwritten, merged-cell tables
- ✅ Dual-engine routing architecture

### Tech Stack
Mistral 7B | Google Cloud Vision | Next.js | PostgreSQL
*(Full stack context → [[tech_stack]])*

### Quote
> "The OCR pipeline handles documents I thought were impossible to automate — handwritten forms, photos of receipts, tables with merged cells. Sub-second. Every time."
> — Head of Finance Ops, Financial Services Client

- ✅ 24/7 availability
- ✅ CRM integrated
