> Research conducted: June 5, 2026.

---

## What Is SITAC?

**Full Name:** Sweden–India Technology and Artificial Intelligence Corridor  
**Signed:** February 25, 2026 at the India AI Impact Summit, New Delhi  
**Parties:** IndiaAI Mission (India) ↔ Business Sweden (Sweden)  
**Type:** Statement of Intent (SoI) — formal framework, not yet a treaty  
**Status:** ACTIVE and expanding. PM Modi visited Sweden in 2026; bilateral trade target doubled to $15.5B (from $7.75B) under Joint Action Plan 2026–2030.

**What it does:** Structured corridor connecting government agencies, industry stakeholders, startups, and academic institutions from both countries to co-develop AI solutions with real industrial and societal outcomes.

8. Watch for **SITAC thematic workshops/conferences** — subscribe to Business Sweden India events calendar and IndiaAI events page.

### Tier 4: Relationship Building (Ongoing)
9. Follow and engage: IndiaAI LinkedIn, Business Sweden India LinkedIn, Sofia Högman posts.
10. Publish content positioning Buteforce as the "precision industrial AI company from India" — exactly the profile SITAC wants to showcase.
