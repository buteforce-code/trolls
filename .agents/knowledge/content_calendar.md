## LinkedIn Posting Cadence
**Platform rule (2026 algo):** Post from Dhyan's personal profile ONLY (@dhyankarthik). Company pages get ~5% feed reach.
**External links:** NEVER in the post body — put buteforce.com links in the FIRST COMMENT only. In-post links reduce reach by 40%.
**Best format:** Native carousels (6.60% avg engagement rate — highest of all formats). Dhyan designs these.

| Day | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Text post (150–300 words) | Story, case study, or insight. No link in post. |
| Wednesday | Carousel (4–8 slides) | Designed in Canva, uploaded as native document. Link in first comment. |
| Friday | Short question or observation | Drives comments → boosts depth score → algorithmic reach |
