## Competitor Categories

### 1. Large AI Consultancies
*Examples: Accenture AI, Deloitte Digital, IBM iX*
- **Their strength:** Brand trust, enterprise relationships, large teams
- **Their weakness:** Slow (6–12 months), expensive, template-heavy, platform lock-in
- **Our counter:** Faster (2–6 weeks), custom-built, client owns everything

### 2. No-Code / Low-Code Agencies
*Examples: Zapier-focused agencies, Make/n8n resellers*
- **Their strength:** Cheap, fast for simple workflows
- **Their weakness:** Can't handle vision, custom models, or complex AI logic
- **Our counter:** Real AI — custom models trained on client data, not workflow glue

### 3. Offshore Dev Shops doing "AI"
*Examples: Generic software houses adding AI to their service menu*
- **Their strength:** Low price point
- **Their weakness:** No deep AI expertise, prototype quality, poor handover
- **Our counter:** AI-first, production-grade, measurable outcomes agreed upfront

### 4. Niche AI SaaS Products
*Examples: Document AI SaaS platforms, generic vision APIs*
- **Their strength:** Plug-and-play, low onboarding friction
- **Their weakness:** Generic models, no workflow integration, endless recurring cost
- **Our counter:** Trained on client's own data, fully integrated, one-time build

## Battle Cards

### "We're talking to a bigger agency"
> "Larger agencies build for their portfolio, not your workflow. We build exactly what you need — and you own it outright. Our projects ship in 2–6 weeks, not 6–12 months."

### "Can't we just use an off-the-shelf tool?"
> "Off-the-shelf tools are built for average use cases. Your workflow isn't average — that's why it's still being done by hand. We train on your data for results generic tools can't match."

### "We tried AI before and it didn't work"
> "Most AI projects fail because someone built the wrong thing correctly. We spend real time understanding the actual problem before writing any code. Measurable outcomes agreed before we start."

### "It's too expensive"
> "Manual processes have a cost too — you just don't see it as a line item. We can calculate the ROI with you before you commit to anything. That's what the [[marketing_engine#Lead Sources]] free audit is for."

### "We don't have the data to train an AI"
> "We've heard this before. In most cases, you have more usable data than you think — and for computer vision, we help you collect what's needed. We'll tell you exactly what's required upfront."
