# Canva Design Briefs — Buteforce

> Asset specs and layout direction for all social graphics.
> Brand colours and anti-patterns → [[brand_bible#Website Design Language]]
> Which posts use which assets → [[content_calendar#Q2 2026 — 30-Day LinkedIn Plan]]

> ⚠️ NOTE: As of 2026-04-12, buteforce.com switched to light theme.
> Accent colour updated to Yellow `#FFFC01` — see [[brand_bible#Website Design Language]] for authoritative colours.
> The brand tokens below reflect the current site design.

---

## Brand Tokens for Canva
- **Primary font:** Inter or DM Sans
- **Background (dark assets):** `#0A0A0A`
- **Background (light assets):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F9FAFB`
- **Accent:** `#FFFC01` (Yellow — use sparingly, key callouts only)
- **Text on dark:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text on light:** `#0A0A0A`

---

## Asset 1: LinkedIn Stat Card
**Size:** 1200 × 628px
**Use:** Announcing a proof point or case study metric → [[case_studies#Proof Points Summary]]
**Layout:**
- Dark background (`#0A0A0A`)
- Large metric centred in white, huge font (e.g. "99.2%")
- Sub-label below in yellow (`#FFFC01`) — "classification accuracy"
- Context line below in gray — "YOLOv8 | Live manufacturing line"
- Buteforce logo bottom-left
- URL bottom-right: buteforce.com

---

## Asset 2: LinkedIn Carousel Cover
**Size:** 1080 × 1080px
**Use:** Educational series, case study threads
**Layout:**
- Dark bg, bold white headline top-left
- Yellow accent line/bar on left edge
- Series indicator top-right (e.g. "1 / 5")
- Buteforce logo bottom-right

---

## Asset 3: Carousel Inner Slide
**Size:** 1080 × 1080px
**Layout:**
- White or `#F9FAFB` background
- Section number top-left in yellow circle
- Heading in dark (`#0A0A0A`), large
- 3–4 bullet points max
- Buteforce mark bottom-right (small)

## Asset 4: Blog Featured Image
**Size:** 1200 × 630px

- Post title in white on dark side

- buteforce.com bottom-left
