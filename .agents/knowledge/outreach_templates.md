## Rules for All Outreach
- Lead with their pain, not our service
- Use one specific proof point relevant to their industry → [[case_studies#Proof Points Summary]]
- One clear CTA — the Free AI Audit (buteforce.com/lp/ai-audit)
- Keep under 120 words
- No "I hope this email finds you well"
- Max 3 follow-ups per lead before marking cold → [[marketing_engine#Campaign Rules]]
