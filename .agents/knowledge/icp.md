## Overview
- **Company size:** 5–200 employees (sweet spot per [[brand_bible#Target Audience]])
- **Revenue:** $1M–$50M
- **Geography:** US, UK, UAE, Australia (English-speaking, high-budget markets)
- **Tech maturity:** Uses cloud tools, some basic automation — NOT AI-native
- **Budget signal:** Has manual workflows costing 10+ hrs/week per person

## ICP by Industry

### 🏭 Manufacturing
- **Pain:** Manual QC, slow inspection, high defect escape rate
- **AI fit:** Computer vision — defect detection, classification, line monitoring
- **Decision maker:** Operations Director, Plant Manager, Head of QC
- **Trigger:** "We inspect everything by hand" / High headcount in QC roles
- **Proof point to use:** 99.2% accuracy, 94% error reduction, 120 items/min
- **Full case study:** [[case_studies#Case Study 1 Computer Vision — Manufacturing QC]]
- **Outreach template:** [[outreach_templates#Manufacturing]]

### 🏠 Real Estate
- **Pain:** Slow lead response, agents buried in repetitive inquiries
- **AI fit:** AI agents — inquiry handling, lead qualification, viewing scheduling
- **Decision maker:** Sales Director, Agency Owner, Head of Operations
- **Trigger:** "We lose leads because we can't respond fast enough"
- **Proof point to use:** 70% inquiries handled autonomously, 95% faster response, 24/7
- **Full case study:** [[case_studies#Case Study 3 AI Agent — Real Estate]]
- **Outreach template:** [[outreach_templates#Real Estate]]

### 💰 Financial Services / Accounting
- **Pain:** Manual document processing, data entry from invoices/receipts/forms
- **AI fit:** Document AI & OCR — structured data extraction at scale
- **Decision maker:** Head of Finance Ops, CFO, Operations Manager
- **Trigger:** "Our team spends hours keying in data from documents"
- **Proof point to use:** Sub-second latency, any document format, lowest CER
- **Full case study:** [[case_studies#Case Study 2 Document AI — OCR Pipeline]]
- **Outreach template:** [[outreach_templates#Financial Services Document Processing]]

### 🚚 Logistics & Supply Chain
- **Pain:** Manual tracking, exception handling, shipment documentation
- **AI fit:** Automation workflows, document processing, escalation agents
- **Decision maker:** COO, Logistics Manager, Head of Operations
- **Trigger:** "Everything goes through email and spreadsheets"
- **Proof point to use:** 80% average time saved
- **Outreach template:** [[outreach_templates#Logistics Operations]]

### 🏥 Healthcare / Professional Services
- **Pain:** Form processing, patient intake, repetitive admin
- **AI fit:** Document AI, workflow automation, scheduling agents
- **Decision maker:** Practice Manager, COO, Head of Admin
- **Trigger:** "Our admin team is drowning in paperwork"
- **Proof point to use:** 80% time saved, 70% tasks handled autonomously

---

## Disqualifiers ❌
- No existing digital processes (nothing to automate)
- Want consulting/advice only — no implementation intent
- Zero budget, no defined workflow problem
- Expecting off-the-shelf setup, not custom builds

---

## Buying Triggers (watch for these signals)
- Hiring for a role that could be automated
- Complaints about speed, errors, or headcount cost
- Competitor recently adopted AI
- Growth has outpaced team capacity
- LinkedIn posts about manual processes or tool frustrations
