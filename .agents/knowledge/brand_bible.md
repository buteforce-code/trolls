# Brand Bible — Buteforce

> The single source of truth for Buteforce brand identity. All agents generating content for this brand must treat this as gospel.
> Dhyan's personal taste that informs these rules → [[dhyan_psychology#Aesthetic Preferences]]

---

## The Name
**Buteforce** — a deliberate misspelling of "Brute Force." The name signals relentless execution and raw engineering power. It is aggressive on purpose.

**The Tension:** The name sounds intimidating/chaotic. The tagline must counterbalance this.

---

## Tagline
> **Precision AI Systems**

**Why this works:**
- "Precision" — surgical, controlled, the opposite of chaotic. Counterbalances "Brute"
- "AI Systems" — not "AI solutions" or "AI tools" — *systems* implies engineered infrastructure with longevity
- Together: We hit hard AND we hit accurately

**Deprecated taglines:**
- ~~"AI Automation & Computer Vision"~~ — too literal, sounds like a service list
- ~~"No consultants. No pilot projects. Working systems."~~ — this is a positioning *statement*, not a tagline. Keep as a sub-headline.

---

## Manifesto (Core Positioning)
```
No consultants. No pilot projects. Working systems.
Numbers over adjectives — 99.2% is a claim. "High accuracy" is not.
We build the AI systems that take it off their plate.
```

This is not optional copy. It is the brand's ethical charter. Every page must communicate this in some form.

## Target Audience

### Primary ICP (Ideal Client Profile)
- **Company size:** 5–200 employees
- **Geography:** US, UK, UAE, Australia (English-speaking, high-budget markets)
- **Problem:** Manual, repetitive processes burning engineer time (document processing, data extraction, operations at scale)
- **Budget signals:** Already paying for SaaS tools; frustrated they don't work for their edge case
- **Decision maker:** CEO, CTO, or Head of Operations
- **NOT target:** Early-stage startups with no budget, companies wanting to "explore AI" with no commitment
- Lead data sourced from → [[marketing_engine]]

## Website Design Language

> Full technical implementation details in [[tech_stack]]

- **Mode:** ~~Dark only. No light mode planned.~~ Light theme always. (Updated: 2026-04-12)
- **Background:** ~~Near-black (not pure `#000` — use very dark grays: `#0a0a0a` or `#0d0d0d`)~~ Clean white or off-white (`#ffffff` or `#f9fafb`) for maximum legibility.
- **Accent color:** Yellow `#FFFC01` — used sparingly. Only for key callouts, highlights, hover states.
- **Typography:**
  - Display: Bold, tight tracking, large size for headlines
  - Body: Clean, readable, high contrast
  - Labels/data: Monospaced (e.g. `font-mono`) for credibility
- **Animation:** All key transitions use Framer Motion. Nothing feels static.
- **Glassmorphism:** Used for proof/stat containers, cards. Strategy: `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-xl border border-white/10`
- **Imagery:** Either bespoke AI-generated or real product screenshots (never stock photos)

## Anti-Patterns (Never Do This)
- No "Book a free call!" CTAs — sounds desperate
- No rainbow gradients or neon colors
- No corporate stock imagery (handshakes, laptops, etc.)
- No vague claims ("cutting-edge AI," "state-of-the-art models")
- No social proof from anonymous sources — only verifiable testimonials
