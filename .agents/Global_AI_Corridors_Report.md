## Cross-Cutting Positioning Strategy

### The Single Narrative That Works Across ALL Programs

Every program above — regardless of country — is looking for the same thing: **a credible Indian AI company with deployed production systems in industrial sectors, not a POC factory**.

Buteforce's positioning should be identical across every program:

> "We are a Chennai-based AI systems company. We build and deploy industrial AI — computer vision for manufacturing QC and retail analytics — in production, not as pilots. 10+ systems live. Our manufacturing clients saw [X]% error reduction in month one."

This works for:
- Business Sweden (Swedish manufacturing companies in India)
- Fraunhofer (German precision manufacturing)
- JETRO (Japanese manufacturing in Tamil Nadu)
- KOTRA (Hyundai Chennai is literally next door)
- NASSCOM (any Indian enterprise buyer)
- Google Accelerator (Physical AI track)

### The Chennai Geographic Advantage (Underutilized)

Chennai is India's manufacturing capital. It has:
- **Hyundai** plant in Sriperumbudur (~45 min from Chennai)
- **Samsung** and other Korean suppliers
- **Renault-Nissan** plant
- **BMW** assembly
- **Michelin** (French) — 2 plants in Tamil Nadu
- **Ashok Leyland** (UK-origin, Indian brand)
- **Several Japanese auto suppliers**

Every bilateral program above (Japan, Korea, France, Germany, Sweden) has a local Chennai/Tamil Nadu angle. Buteforce should be pitching itself as "the AI company for Chennai's manufacturing corridor" — not just "an Indian AI company." This is concrete, local, and immediately relevant.
